NPI checksum validator

Paste a 10-digit National Provider Identifier and get an instant checksum verdict. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. A valid checksum means the number is well-formed, not that it's assigned: confirm assignment in the official NPPES NPI Registry.

The check digit is computed with the Luhn algorithm over the "80840" health-industry prefix plus the first 9 digits, doubling from the rightmost payload digit (CMS NPI check-digit specification).

Why claims bounce on NPI errors

Most keying errors produce an NPI that fails the checksum — clearinghouses and payers reject these before adjudication, and the denial often surfaces days later as a batch rejection. Validating at data-entry time is the cheapest denial you'll ever prevent.

Know what the check digit cannot see. It is a Luhn mod-10 checksum, and Luhn does not catch every transposition. It catches a swap of two adjacent digits unless those digits are a 0 and a 9; it catches no swap of two digits an even number of places apart. So a passing checksum means the number is well formed, not that it is the right provider — always confirm the NPI is actually assigned, to the provider you mean, in the NPPES registry.
