What happens next

We reply to agree on scope and sign a Business Associate Agreement — the HIPAA paperwork that has to exist before any patient information changes hands. Only then does your office manager export 60 days of already-billed claims and remittances (a CSV from your system, or the 835 files from your clearinghouse). You get a written scorecard back: the denial patterns in your own data, which claims are at risk, and what we'd do differently. It's yours to keep whether or not we ever work together.

What we don't do

We don't promise higher reimbursement — no honest billing service can, because your codes are determined by your documentation and your payers' rules. We don't touch your money: payers remit directly to your accounts. And no claim goes out without a licensed human reviewing and signing it.
