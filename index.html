How it works

A human signs every claim

Software assists; a person is accountable. Nothing goes to a payer without human review and sign-off — permanently, not just while the technology matures.

Your money never touches our hands

Payers remit directly to your clinic's accounts. We prepare, submit, and work the claims; the revenue path stays yours end to end.

Coded down, never up

The engine codes from what's documented — and only what's documented. When documentation is ambiguous, it selects the lower, defensible code and flags the gap for the physician instead of guessing upward.

No platform to install

We work from your existing EHR's exports and established clearinghouse rails. No new software for your staff, no migration project, no lock-in.

No billing service can honestly guarantee higher reimbursement, and we don't.
