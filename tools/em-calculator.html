2021 E/M office-visit level calculator

Office/outpatient codes 99202–99215 under the AMA's 2021 rules: select the level by total time on the date of the encounter or by medical decision making (2 of 3 elements) — whichever your documentation supports. Thresholds below are the AMA's, cited at the bottom. Educational use only; no patient data enters this page (everything runs in your browser).

Count the physician's/QHP's own face-to-face and non-face-to-face time on that date. Excludes clinical-staff time, separately reported services, travel, and general teaching.

Medical decision making — optional

Only problems addressed at this encounter count. A stable chronic illness means at treatment goal; "uncontrolled" and "controlled" diabetes are the same single condition.

A lab panel (e.g., basic metabolic panel) is a single test; serial results of one test are one test; pulse oximetry doesn't count as a test.

Risk means management decisions at this encounter — including options considered but not selected after shared decision making. A history of a past admission is not a risk element.

The 2021 time bands

New patient: 99202 15–29 · 99203 30–44 · 99204 45–59 · 99205 60–74 minutes.
Established: 99212 10–19 · 99213 20–29 · 99214 30–39 · 99215 40–54 minutes.

Beyond the top band, prolonged services differ by payer: AMA 99417 begins at 75 (new) / 55 (established) minutes; Medicare's G2212 begins only at 89 / 69 minutes (CMS CY2021 PFS final rule).
